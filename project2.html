Overview

Identity, packaging, and go-to-market

Three separate projects, each with its own client and brief: a mock branding concept, a freelance logo and packaging job, and six months as head designer building a startup's brand, product, and merch from scratch.

01 · Concept brand

Gloss Moderne

A mock design for social media branding, focused on clean beauty. I wanted to emulate a minimalist, modest, and luxurious feel to their products that would draw the audience in while fitting with their current theme on social media.

Tools used: Adobe Photoshop, Lummi AI, Unsplash
Vision board & fonts
02 · Freelance visual identity

Logo & Packaging Design

I designed the label and logo for a brown seaweed extract supplement. The client wanted a natural and premium vibe. A hand-drawn seaweed illustration, Japanese and English typography, and a warm paper label

Tools used: Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, Figma
Logo Showcase

The brand's identity revolved around health wellness and serenity. When ideating for the logo, I knew I wanted to represent the brand to show the audience a product with minimal and luxurious branding to demonstrate trust and reflect their purpose seamlessly.
